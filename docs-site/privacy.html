PAM Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Information accessed

With authorization, PAM accesses Google Calendar event information to answer scheduling and availability questions. PAM also processes messages sent to its Telegram chatbot.

How information is used

Calendar information is used to calculate availability and generate relevant responses. PAM currently uses read-only Google Calendar access and does not modify calendar events.

AI processing and memory

PAM uses a third-party AI model service, BharatCode, to process conversational requests and generate responses. PAM also uses Supermemory to store and retrieve conversational memories. Calendar tool outputs are not intentionally stored as durable memories.

Data storage and security

Google authorization credentials are stored in encrypted form. PAM restricts chatbot access to authorized users. Data is processed by the application's hosting infrastructure and relevant service providers.

Sharing

PAM does not sell personal information. Information necessary to provide the assistant's features may be processed by Google, Telegram, BharatCode, Supermemory, and the hosting provider, as applicable.

Retention and deletion

Conversation history is maintained for application functionality, and durable memories may be retained until removed through the application's supported memory operations. Users can request information about deletion through the developer contact.

Revoking Google access

You can revoke PAM's Google access through your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access prevents further authorized Calendar retrieval until you reconnect.

Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, contact the developer using the support email displayed on the OAuth consent screen.
