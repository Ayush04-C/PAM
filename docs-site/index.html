PAM — Personal Assistant

PAM is a personal AI assistant that helps its authorized user understand Google Calendar schedules and availability through a Telegram chatbot.

Features

PAM can read authorized calendar events, summarize schedules, identify free time, and answer natural-language questions. PAM does not create, modify, or delete Google Calendar events.
